# Response to the round-three review of v3.6.0

The round-three review ("PoS Repository Round Three Review") examined the v3.6.0
merge, commit `b6f2eca`, and raised eight findings, H01 to H08. This page gives the
status of each in version 3.7.0. The earlier reviews are answered in
[review_response.md](review_response.md) (R01 to R25) and
[review_response_round2.md](review_response_round2.md) (G01 to G10); the results are
in [recycling_results.md](recycling_results.md).

Every experiment was rerun at 3.7.0 from commit `2ff94e9`; each file records its own
provenance, captured when its command started. The source changed after those runs
only in the help text of `recycling --only`, which now lists the new experiments. Three experiments are new: E1s (detection-side sensitivity, H04),
E1q (quarantining the pseudonym instead of revoking the holder) and E3bi (one insider
at a time). E4 gains a delegated fresh-label control, `fresh_pm`. The suite is 125
tests.

## Findings

| Item | Status | What changed | Effect on the results |
|---|---|---|---|
| H01 Expired messages violate the opportunity hierarchy | Fixed in code | The former holder does not send under a credential whose certificate has expired; the expiry is printed in the certificate it kept, so this uses only information it has. Every key check falls in exactly one bucket, `key_checks = held_by_self + retired_skips + expired_key_seconds + valid_key_seconds`, the retired-label early return under fresh issuance included, and `sent <= eligible <= valid_key_seconds`. Test: `test_opportunity_tiers_are_nested_against_sent_events`, against the messages actually sent, in recycle (S1, S3) and fresh_vgk. | Within 120 s nothing expires and E1 is unchanged in every cell. Over 600 s with 300 s certificates the lifetime now shows as fewer attempts (S3 sends 9,453 instead of 19,014), and acceptance among attempts equals that of longer lifetimes. It also removes a confound: the RSU's ledger check ignores certificate expiry, so expired forgeries had added wrongful revocations. S1 now revokes 84.2 honest vehicles over 600 s at 300 s certificates (3.6.0: 90.5), S2 58.4 (71.2). |
| H02 Receiver and ledger disagree at a handoff endpoint | Fixed | The ledger logs every allotment and return with its time; `owner_at(pid, t)` (binary search) gives the holder and key at a message's own timestamp, with the outgoing holder owning the handoff instant, the convention of the (t_start, t_end] holder windows. The RSU's attribution and clone check use it. A real-transfer test runs rekey rounds, takes a label that passed straight to a new holder, and checks ledger owner, receiver window and verifying key together at the boundary and just after (`test_real_handoff_ledger_window_and_key_agree`). That test found that rekey binding did not carry the vehicle's new key into the ownership log; fixed, with `test_honest_messages_pass_the_rsu_check_in_every_mode`. | E5 and every rekey cell are unchanged. The E5 rekey rows remain sampled attack outcomes, not exhaustive boundary tests: S1 acts on the beacons the holder emits, and the incoming holder is silent at first. |
| H03 Exposure weighting differs from the definition | Fixed | Means pool every window (each window weighs the same) with a bootstrap that resamples seeds. Each archived segment keeps its pseudonym and holder; `span_s` (gap-tolerant, first to last accepted second) and `accepted_s` (distinct accepted seconds) are reported separately; windows that reach the end of the run are `truncated`, and a mean over completed windows is given beside the mean over all. | E1 S3: pooled mean 36.4 s (3.6.0 averaged seed means: 36.8 s), 100 of 174 windows truncated, completed windows 33.2 s. S1's windows are 7 s spans of 7 accepted seconds each. |
| H04 Missing controls affect the headline | Run | E1s: the E1 core cell on the same ten seeds under nine detection-side variants (local or shared RSU histories; batched or continuous processing with a 1 s delay; both; 10 % message loss; clone allowance 10 m and 50 m; signed-position error 3 m and 10 m), each with its own attempt denominators, an attack-free false-positive control and the genuine misbehaver on a workload matched to the forger's (one replay per second). The RSU's clone allowance became a parameter for this. | S1 at 5 % former holders revokes 13 to 43 of 91.5 honest vehicles in two minutes depending on the variant: 43.4 by default, 13.2 with local histories, 42.9 with continuous processing, 12.7 with both; loss, the 10 m allowance and position error leave it unchanged. S2 (8.5) falls to 0 with a 50 m allowance, inside which its 40 m ghost passes. No variant revokes an honest vehicle without attackers; receivers reject 0.13 % of honest receptions as implausible. The matched misbehaver is revoked at 60 s with batched and 35 s with continuous processing. The control also exposed a defect in the local-history option (an RSU's history of a previous holding counted against the label's next holder), fixed with `test_attack_free_runs_revoke_nobody`. |
| H05 No implementable observation interface | Scoped, not changed | The attack schedules still read simulator state, as stated since 3.6.0; the results are positioned as a conditional mechanism study of one architecture, with every rate labelled conditional. An observation-limited former-holder schedule was attempted for this round and not completed; it is not in this version. E3bi runs each insider alone; former-holder CAMs reach the eavesdropper, insider CAMs do not. | The decisive question is answered only in part: the result survives local histories and explicit report timing as a range (13 to 43 honest revocations), with every attempted message accounted for, but not with an observation-limited attacker. |
| H06 Clean-source flag has blind spots | Fixed | The source inventory covers `src`, `config`, `scripts`, the dependency pins (`requirements*.txt`, `requirements.lock`, `requirements-sumo.lock`), `pyproject.toml` and the `Dockerfile`, untracked files included. A differing source is archived (diff of tracked files, full text of untracked ones) under `results/provenance/`, named by its SHA-256. Records say `provenance_capture: "command start"`; 3.6.0 files written before that said nothing and recorded the commit at write time. SUMO results carry the trace content hashes (the same hash as `CONTENT_SHA256SUMS`). Test: `test_provenance_archives_untracked_and_dirty_source`. | All 3.7.0 result files record a clean source. |
| H07 Paper figure regeneration cannot be audited | PENDING | The manuscript figure generator (`make_figures.py`) is to be committed under `paper/` with the figure-number map and a single command. | |
| H08 Tests check local counters, not cross-layer invariants | Fixed | New tests run against real event records: sent messages against the opportunity buckets (H01), an actual handoff against ledger, window and key (H02), honest messages through the RSU check in every mode, attack-free runs under both history modes. Retention is reported and tested per credential version (label, key, expiry) as well as per label; allocation never returns a label to a vehicle that held it, so the two coincide, and the test pins that. The full pytest log with the environment is in `results/test_log.txt`. | |

## Additions both reviews asked for

| Addition | Result |
|---|---|
| Isolated insiders (E3bi) | Each entity forging alone gives the same rates as the joint E3b run within about 3 points on every row: at 40 m a third of receivers accept and the RSU blames the holder for 70 %; within 25 m or during the silent period every receiver accepts and nobody is blamed. A row is now what one compromised entity achieves. |
| Delegated fresh-label control (`fresh_pm`, M07) | Fresh labels, vehicle-generated keys, certificates signed by a PM whose PKI certificate rides along and is checked by receivers. No forgery is possible and no honest vehicle is revoked; the PKI signs nothing and the PMs carry fresh_vgk's PKI signing load (24 CPU-s per 1,000 vehicle-hours with this run's coefficients); privacy is equivalent to recycle within ±1 point; the simulator's per-vehicle byte count rises with the chained certificate (17.7 k against 12.1 k). |
| Quarantine instead of revocation (E1q) | Withdrawing the reported pseudonym and revoking nobody turns S1's 43.4 wrongful revocations in two minutes (90.5 over ten) into none. The cost moves elsewhere: 41 labels withdrawn in two minutes and 208 over ten, 32 and 81 honest vehicles losing a pseudonym, allotment stock-outs rising to 224 per run over ten minutes, and receivers still accepting the forgeries. The attribution policy, not the clone itself, is what turns a clone into a victim. |
